# Risk register of the agentic organisation

**Purpose.** Risks of the organisation itself (not of the product – those are kept by the architect in ADRs and by legal in its registers): each with an owner,
mitigation, **evidence that the mitigation works**, and a gap to close. Starting sources: report `docs/raport/2026-11-09-raport.md` (ch. 6),
research `docs/research/2026-11-09-standardy-organizacji-agentycznych.md`, lessons from the source fleet (November 2026).

**Scale.** P (probability) and S (severity) 1–3 (low / medium / high); score = P × S; **≥ 6 = action this quarter**.
**Statuses:** `otwarte` (open: gap with no action owner) · `mitygowane` (mitigated: mitigation deployed, evidence in the column) · `przyjęte D-…`
(accepted: the owner accepts the residual risk) · `zamknięte` (closed). No personal data.
**Who and when.** Kept by the CEO; **quarterly review** in the first `PEŁNY` (full) run of January, April, July and October (result:
`ceo/analizy/RRRR-QN-ryzyka.md` + items for the owner in `ceo/do-decyzji.md`) and after every incident (`procesy/90-incydent.md`,
step 6 – new risk or changed score). Any agent may add a new risk by an order to the CEO. Changing a status to `przyjęte` – only by
the owner's decision.

## Quarterly review – procedure (CEO)

1. For each row: does the mitigation still exist (file, test, hook – checked at the source, not from memory); numbers for the quarter
   (incidents `incydenty/`, hook blocks `bezpieczenstwo/blokady.jsonl` per rule, cost from `pm/narzedzia/przebieg-raport.py podsumuj 90`,
   KPI F1–F12) → new P × S score.
2. Gaps scoring ≥ 6 → orders with a deadline in this quarter (Issue `agent:<właściciel>`); no action owner = status `otwarte`
   and an item for the owner in `ceo/do-decyzji.md` (risk acceptance `przyjęte D-…` or a budget for mitigation).
3. New risks from the quarter's incidents (runbook step 6) and from research (fleet practices) – added with the next ID.
4. Result: `ceo/analizy/RRRR-QN-ryzyka.md` (table of score changes, list of actions), one sentence in the report for the owner; `zamknięte` risks
   stay in the table for 4 quarters with the closure date, then move to the archive section below.
