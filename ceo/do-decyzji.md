# For the owner's decision – one list

Kept by the CEO agent. Only matters not settled by `decyzje.md`, `zasady.md`, `uprawnienia.yaml` or the agent's
scope. Most important at the top. Every matter has a recommendation and the consequence of delay – the owner answers "yes / no /
otherwise: …". After the decision: a `D-…` entry in `decyzje.md`, the item removed from here, orders to the agents.

The default answer after the deadline passes is **"no"** (the safe option) – the CEO plans so that a missing answer
does not block the organisation.
