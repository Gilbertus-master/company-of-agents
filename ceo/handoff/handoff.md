# Handoff – state of the organisation

„STAN PRZY PRZENIESIENIU” (state at hand-over) sections are appended by the CEO before the fleet is moved to another account (change of operator or plan –
`ceo/handoff/START-nowe-konto.md`) or before a planned break. Newest at the bottom. An exhausted limit is not a reason
to move – the fleet waits for the reset or works on paid overage (`ceo/handoff/konto-floty.md`).
