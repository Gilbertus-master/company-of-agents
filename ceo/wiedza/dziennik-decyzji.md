# CEO decision log – prediction (%) and check

Every recommendation to the owner and every CEO ruling gets a row with a **checkable prediction** (what will be true on the check date) and a probability.
After the check date the CEO records the outcome (yes / no / don't know – evidence). Calibration (Brier = mean squared error) is computed after ≥ 4 weeks in the quarterly review;
before that there is too little data. A ruling reversed by the owner = a separate column (shows where the CEO exceeds its mandate or misreads the owner's will). No personal data.
